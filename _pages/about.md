Research Statement
======
My current work focuses on:
* Parameter efficient fine-tuning of large models (PEFT).
* Acceleration of the Diffusion and Flow-Matching Model, including image generation, image restoration, and the VLA models.

**For partial differential equations, especially nonlinear evolution equations, the evolution of the solution is often highly dependent on the initial state. Therefore, starting from an initial state that is closer to the target solution or more consistent with the prior structure of the system often leads to more stable, more accurate, or better final results.**
