This preprint presents ScaleResfusion, a real-world image-restoration framework built on pretrained text-to-image rectified-flow models. It combines residual transport, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and knowledge distillation to improve restoration efficiency.

The publication page links to the arXiv record and the [official implementation](https://github.com/YukinoshitaLove/ScaleResfusion).
